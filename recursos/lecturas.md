## Go

- *A Tour of Go* — https://go.dev/tour (módulo 02; hazlo completo).
- *Effective Go* — https://go.dev/doc/effective_go
- *Go by Example* — https://gobyexample.com (consulta rápida).
- *Go Code Review Comments* — https://go.dev/wiki/CodeReviewComments
- Documentación de módulos (incluye `GOPRIVATE`) — https://go.dev/ref/mod
- Blog oficial (https://go.dev/blog): "Working with Errors in Go 1.13", "Go Concurrency Patterns: Context", "Pipelines and cancellation".

## Gin, GORM, Swagger
